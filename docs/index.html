Local-first stress support

One steady step when stress gets loud.

ClearSpace offers a calm SOS flow, paced breathing, grounding, local personalization, optional scripted voice guidance, and gentle offline prompts without accounts, telemetry, cloud sync, or AI in the critical support path.

Open app source Read project notes

Fast SOS

A dominant first action leads to scripted grounding and a choice of breathing or 5-4-3-2-1 support.

Private by default

Preferences, support contact, toolkit items, and journal notes stay in browser local storage.

Careful expansion

AI and speech input are not connected. The current voice guide uses optional local browser speech output for fixed scripts.

ClearSpace is a support tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose symptoms, prescribe treatment, or replace emergency care.
